We combine foundry experience with in-house model making, 3D sand printing and digital measurement technology. This allows us to support projects from CAD data to the finished casting.

## From CAD data to the finished casting

Our services work together as one process chain:

- support in development and CAD design
- 3D CAD data processing
- in-house EPS model making
- production of sand molds and cores
- direct production of molds and cores by 3D sand printing
- aluminium sand casting for single parts and small batches
- 3D scanning for quality control and reverse engineering

## Flexible combinations for special requirements

For special applications, we combine individual processes as required. Examples include castings with integrated stainless-steel tubes for temperature control or tool-less sand molds for prototypes and single parts.
